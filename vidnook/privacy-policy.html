All Video Downloader – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

All Video Downloader ("the app") is a web browser with a download manager and a video player for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and what leaves your device.

Summary

  • There is no account and no sign-up. We do not run a server that receives your browsing or your videos.
  • Your history, bookmarks, downloads, settings and private-folder PIN stay on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising identifiers as described below.

Information stored only on your device

DataWhy
Browsing history, bookmarks, cookies and site dataTo make the built-in browser work. You can erase them in Settings → Clear browsing data.
Videos you save, their titles and the page they came fromTo list and play your downloads. Videos are saved to Movies/AllVideoDownloader or to the app's private folder.
Private-folder PINStored only as a salted hash, never as the PIN itself.
Settings and purchase statusTo remember your choices and whether ads are removed.

This information is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes it, except for videos you saved to your gallery.

Websites you visit

When you browse or download, the app connects directly to the websites you choose, like any browser. Those websites receive your IP address, cookies and the usual browser information, and handle it under their own privacy policies.

Advertising (free version)

The free version uses Google AdMob to show ads. Google may collect and process the advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and ad interaction data, in order to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where required, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Buying the one-time "Remove ads" upgrade turns ads off.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state – to browse and download, and to honour the "Wi-Fi only" setting.
  • Notifications and foreground service – to keep downloads running and show their progress.
  • Storage (Android 9 only) – to save videos to the Movies folder. Newer Android versions do not need it.

Acceptable use and copyright

All Video Downloader is a general-purpose tool. Only save content that you own or are permitted to download. Saving from YouTube is not supported. If you are a rights holder and believe the app is being used to infringe your rights, contact us at the address below and we will respond promptly.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
